fix(i18n): give Indonesian its own day-period buckets

The English engine marks 05:00-11:59 as morning, which is wrong for Indonesian
(03:00 is still "pagi", 11:00 is "siang", 15:00 is "sore"). The Indonesian
formatter now derives pagi/siang/sore/malam from the real 24h hour, and the test
covers each boundary.
